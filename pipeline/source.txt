🪔 KOLKATA DURGA PUJA PANDAL
HOPPING GUIDE 2026
By Travel With Preet

Kolkata has hundreds of Durga Pujas — and the biggest mistake you can make is randomly
travelling from one end of the city to another.

So we’ve put together our pandal-hopping guide covering 227 pandals, organised into:

📍 Area-wise routes​
🚶 Walking circuits​
🚇 Metro-wise routes​
✨ North, Central, South, South-West, East & Port Kolkata​
💡 Practical Puja tips
Our biggest recommendation: Pick ONE area/cluster at a time. You’ll spend less time
travelling and more time actually enjoying Pujo.

Esplanade is an important Blue ↔ Green Line interchange, making it useful when moving

But our advice?

Don't spend your Pujo night changing areas constantly.

One good circuit > 10 random pandals across Kolkata.

🗓️ HOW WE WOULD PLAN MULTIPLE
PUJO NIGHTS
If you have several evenings in Kolkata, instead of trying to see everything together:

Night 1: Bagbazar + Shyambazar + Hatibagan + Kumartuli​
Night 2: Dum Dum + Lake Town + Sreebhumi​
Night 3: Kalighat + Rashbehari + Ballygunge​
Night 4: Naktala + Garia + Patuli​
Night 5: Salt Lake + New Town​
Night 6: Behala + Barisha

This way every night has its own vibe and you aren't spending half your Pujo sitting in traffic.




⚠️ PUJA SURVIVAL TIPS
1. Start earlier than everyone else.​
If possible, start in the afternoon. Crowds increase massively after sunset.
2. Metro > car for many routes.​
Road closures, barricades and traffic diversions are common during Pujo.

3. Don't depend on one exact walking path.​
Police may create one-way pedestrian routes depending on crowds.

4. Wear comfortable shoes.​
You will walk MUCH more than you expect.

5. Carry water — but travel light.​
Avoid carrying unnecessary bags when entering crowded pandals.

6. Keep your phone charged.​
Screenshots of routes + a power bank can save you when networks become congested.

7. Don't chase numbers.​
Seeing 8–10 pandals properly is better than rushing through 25 just to tick them off.

8. Check Metro timings before leaving.​
Special Puja services and late-night timings can change each year/day. Check the latest official
schedule before depending on an overnight Metro.

9. Expect queues at the famous pandals.​
Especially Sreebhumi and the major North/South Kolkata names.

10. Keep buffer time.​
A route that looks short on Maps can take much longer during Pujo.




❤️ OUR BIGGEST PUJO TIP
Don't make Kolkata Durga Puja a checklist.

Pick an area.

Walk through the lanes.

Stop for puchka.

Have a roll.

Listen to the dhaak.

Watch the crowds.
Do some pandal hopping.

And enjoy the madness.

Because Pujo in Kolkata isn't only about how many pandals you managed to cover.

It's about everything that happens between them.   ❤️
